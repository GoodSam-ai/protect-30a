Add your voice

This is where residents of the 30A corridor turn concern into action. Pledge your support, send a message to the people who decide, RSVP for upcoming meetings, and pick up ready-to-use tools for your household and your HOA. Use the links below to reach the available tools and source records.

Take the pledge

Show support using the existing voluntary pledge form. Open the pledge form →

Contact decision-makers

Personalize the stormwater letter before opening your email app. Open the letter builder → For current advocacy issues, visit Raven or Helen McCall Park.

RSVP for a meeting

Check the official meeting notice before making plans. Open Walton County agendas and notices → Proposed milestones are not confirmed hearings.

Meetings & events calendar

Meeting dates and venues can change. Use Walton County’s official website for the current agenda and public notice. Explore the community podcast series →

HOA & resident toolkit

Share the stormwater plan, its source library, and your community page. Ask neighbors to describe dated observations and distinguish proposals from approved projects.

Community sponsors

Sponsorship supports independent community outreach. Ask about sponsorship → It does not imply government endorsement.
