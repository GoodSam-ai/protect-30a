Raven

Protect the Bay. Prevent More Flooding.

What is proposed

The applicant describes replacing the driving range and practice facilities with 22 detached homes and an interior road, with 24 villas contemplated in a later phase. This proposal does not establish permanent closure of the entire golf course.

Read the applicant narrative

What still needs an answer

  1. Which parcels supply the development rights, and does the final ledger reconcile?
  2. Which open-space reduction applies—6.3 or 9.52 acres—and what remains protected?
  3. Which land-use labels and standards control the review?
  4. What would be approved now, and what remains conceptual?
  5. Where does runoff discharge, and what do the models show for neighboring properties and receiving waters?

The county staff report identifies conflicting figures and unresolved review items. These warrant written answers; discrepancies alone do not prove wrongdoing.

Read the county staff report

Protect the water that connects us

Good Samaritan Institute testing reported PFAS in one pooled sample of 13 catfish livers collected at Ditch #10 in Santa Rosa Beach. This is an existing watershed concern—not a finding that Raven caused contamination. It does not measure edible fillets or establish a hydrologic connection to this project.

Our request: verify the drainage pathway, assess potential pollutant mobilization, and establish appropriate baseline testing before disturbance where supported by the evidence.

Read the full laboratory report

What the community is asking for

  • A complete corrected record with time for public review.
  • Independent drainage analysis covering volume, peak flow, water levels and flood duration.
  • Receiving-water protection supported by site-specific evidence.
  • Enforceable monitoring, maintenance and corrective-action duties.
  • Continuance while material questions remain; denial if applicable standards cannot be met.

Approval status: Hearing date and final decision status are not confirmed. An agenda request for approval is not an approval.
