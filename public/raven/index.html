Current performance needs its own evidence

Drainage: compare existing/proposed peak flow, runoff volume, water level and inundation duration using approved criteria, native models and accepted as-builts. Map shared assets and outfalls before transferring Driftwood conclusions to Raven; aerial proximity does not establish a hydraulic connection.

Utilities: distinguish permitted/design capacity, actual average/peak demand, committed future demand and local pipe/lift-station/distribution capacity. A plant annual average cannot clear local wet-weather or pressure constraints. Current permits, reservations, seasonal records and accepted asset drawings remain needed.

Reported leaks: a concern requiring verification, not an established condition or health-danger finding. Seek dated asset/location records, repairs, pressure logs, overflow reports, sampling, advisory issue/rescission and regulator closure. A leak report does not establish contamination.

Favorable evidence must remain visible

SWUCI’s 2025 report p. 3 reports a July TTHM sample of 170 ppb at SS #24, but explains the site annual average did not result in an MCL violation. Location relative to Raven and any relation to leaks are unverified. An annual report does not resolve every localized 2026 incident.

Harper, printed p. 31 / PDF p. 35, finds historical DRI deficiencies alongside favorable interior-system county-standard and shared-outfall conclusions. DEP correspondence AO01 p. 1 reports inspected pond compliance. Neither is blanket Raven clearance.

Find the promise before judging the loss

Private golf access, public recreation concurrency and DRI open space are distinct. Match the specific practice-facility conversion to adopted Map H/Exhibit E, acreage definitions, recorded rights/amendments and replacement conditions. The 6.3- versus 9.52-acre comparison is an accounting question, not proof of unlawful loss.

AO13 p. 2 concerns a historical Parcel 561 plat/master-plan issue, not current Raven obligations. AO09’s reversal/remand does not invalidate all approvals or establish public golf-access rights.
