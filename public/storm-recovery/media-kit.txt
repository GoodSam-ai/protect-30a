Walton Community Cleanup Pilot
October 22, 2026 • America/Chicago

Eight communities. One coordinated cleanup.

SOCIAL POST
Eight communities. One coordinated cleanup. Protect30A District Ops is organizing Community Storm Recovery, with a proposed first pilot along Highway 393 and in Bellamar/Gulf Place. The goal: professional cleanup, clear costs, documented work, and responsible reuse of suitable wood. Partner participation, funding, pricing, and start dates are still being confirmed.
https://protect30a.org/storm-recovery

HOA BULLETIN
Protect30A District Ops invites HOA and resident representatives to help shape a coordinated storm debris cleanup pilot. Share your neighborhood’s needs and help organize priorities. Every participating property would require permission, a defined scope, and an approved payment arrangement before work. The pilot is organizing; bookings and financial assistance are not yet confirmed.

PARTNER OVERVIEW
Proposed structure: one willing lead administrator, eight separately tracked community budgets, professional cleanup contractors, and District Ops evidence tracking. All participation, award eligibility, procurement, pricing, and payment terms require confirmation. No affiliation with FEMA or USDA is implied.
