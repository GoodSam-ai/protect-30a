November 2 web addendum: the original downloadable meeting brief remains a dated v1.0 document. This newly located correspondence adds context to its unresolved questions. No new agency response is implied.

Verified public records: The county posts 2022–2023 and 2023–2024 MS4 reports. The latter covers October 15, 2023–October 14, 2024. Public posting does not prove DEP receipt. DEP generally requires annual reports in the first five-year cycle, then reports in years two and four unless it requires more frequent reporting. Sources 1–3.

Not yet verified: Current coverage and renewal status, applicable filing deadlines, DEP receipt of required filings, and outstanding deficiencies. The June 2025 county statement is summarized above; the underlying DEP instruction and current disposition remain unverified. This brief does not establish a permit violation.

1 · Define exactly what is overdue

“When staff said we were not up to date, precisely what was overdue?”

Request: Each missed permit condition, filing, inspection, maintenance task or corrective action; its original deadline; the date staff identified it; and its current status. Distinguish paperwork gaps from unfinished field work.

2 · Prove coverage and required submissions

“Show us current permit coverage and what DEP actually received.”

Request: Permit number and coverage dates; DEP acknowledgment; renewal Notice of Intent; current Stormwater Management Program; and required reports for the current and preceding permit cycles, with due dates, submission receipts and DEP deficiency correspondence. Explain years when no report was required.

3 · Show the work and the correction plan

“What records support the reported work, and what remains unresolved?”

Request: Asset and outfall maps; pond and inspection logs; illicit-discharge and construction-enforcement records; maintenance work orders; and an unresolved-problem list showing location, ownership, responsible department, interim action, cost and target completion date. Include a shared mosquito-control referral and closure log; distinguish mosquito findings from permit violations.

The requested outcome

“Please deliver a written status table within 10 business days: obligation, due date, evidence, gap, owner and correction date. Who owns delivery?”

This is a proposed management deadline, not a statutory public-records deadline. Request an estimate before chargeable records work.

Responsibility check: The 2023–2024 report names Melinda Gates as program contact and Buddy Wright as responsible authority at that time. Confirm today’s assignments, including Scott Caraway’s role; do not infer current duties from the older report. Source 2.
