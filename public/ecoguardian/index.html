The proposal

Bring thoughtful, small-scale restoration closer to home—with suitable sites, qualified design and a plan for lasting care.

From idea to stewardship

Plan carefully.
Build responsibly.

The proposed pilot starts with community cleanup and site assessment, with bioswales, rain gardens and native planting considered as technical approvals and funding allow. Each project would need technical review, site permission and an approved funding route before work begins.

01

Listen & assess

Understand local priorities. Plan permitted cleanups, then review runoff, soils, access and maintenance needs. Identify sites where a small intervention could be appropriate.

02

Design & deliver

Confirm leadership and funding. Develop suitable designs, secure required approvals and document the scope before installation.

03

Care & learn

Set clear maintenance responsibilities. Record baseline conditions, completion and follow-up observations. Share what the evidence shows.
